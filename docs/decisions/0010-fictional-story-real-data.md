# 0010. The Academy story is fictional; its data is real

**Decision.** The Academy follows a made-up lander, Pip, to the extreme south pole in
2028. Sites, terrain, and Sun and Earth positions are real. Pip uses the IM-2 Athena
preset. The finale compares the learner's choice with NASA's Artemis candidate regions.

**Why.** We considered retelling Chandrayaan-3. It landed at 69°S, where the Sun climbs to
about 20°. The challenge is about the extreme pole, where it stays under 2°. A fictional
mission lets the story sit where the problem is hardest, and lets the learner make a real
choice with no known answer to copy.

**Real missions still appear** as presets, as catalog sites, and as short true stories
inside lessons: Athena's tip-over and VIPER's 50-hour rule.
